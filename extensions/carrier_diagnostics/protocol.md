# Additional diagnostic protocol

Written before inspecting additional diagnostic estimates, September 27, 2026. This is a contemporaneous work plan, not historical preregistration.

All analyses use the same Borenstein 2005–2010 extract and original 2007 eligibility/classification. Original source files and existing results are read-only. Every requested model is retained irrespective of sign or significance.

1. Split the valid-distance expanded panel into the original 156 overlap routes, the 104 legacy comparison routes, and the additional comparison routes. Southwest presence is any positive 2007 passenger count on an itinerary recording WN in either carrier field. Its share divides that count by all sampled 2007 journeys. Report route-mean characteristics and both route-mean and passenger-weighted WN shares. WN is a specific carrier indicator, not an exhaustive low-cost-carrier classification.
2. Report quarterly mean log fares and indices relative to 2007Q4, with available route counts. Also report a balanced 24-quarter cohort to separate route entry/exit from within-route changes. Summarize 2007 versus 2009–2010, excluding transition year 2008, without attributing differences to Southwest.
3. Estimate the already specified distance-bin and baseline one-coupon-share-bin by quarter model in both legacy and expanded samples, adding (a) baseline WN presence by quarter and (b) fixed WN share categories: zero, positive below 50%, at least 50%. Keep the underlying route samples unchanged. Run the presence model's event-study pretest as a diagnostic. The rules are not tuned to pass a pretest.
4. On the same fixed 260-route legacy-valid panel, classify individual aggregate source records as DL/NW single-carrier, other single-carrier, or mixed-carrier. Single-carrier uses the production rule: one coupon (cop=0), or equal carrier codes. Mixed itineraries, including DL/NW combinations and regional codes, are never reassigned to the merging group. Retain positive finite passengers and fare; average fares are passenger weighted within group-route-quarter. Report mixed-group shares and unavailable cells.
5. Within overlap routes with positive DL/NW and rival single-carrier traffic in all four 2007 quarters, estimate a descriptive route-fixed-effects quarterly log-fare gap, DL/NW minus rivals, normalized to 2007Q4. Report the linear contrast averaging 2009–2010 versus averaging 2007 with route-clustered uncertainty. Also report a 24-quarter paired-cell balanced cohort sensitivity, and a two-coupon-only paired-cohort check for product composition. Do not claim those gaps identify treatment or rivals' strategic response. A comparison-group difference-in-differences of these gaps is undefined in 2007 because the original control definition excludes DL/NW.
6. Verify source aggregates against existing route-quarter passenger and revenue totals and independently recalculate selected cells directly from raw rows. Preserve all positive cells and report any exclusions. Do not use findings to reselect bins, routes, or main specifications.
