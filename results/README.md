# Saved current results

The current `extended_results.json` and `model_registry.csv` were generated by `analysis/extend_analysis.py`, then independently checked. They are the numerical source for the paper's tables and figures.

- `model_registry.csv`: every pooled/event coefficient, uncertainty interval, target term and sample count.
- `extended_results.json`: complete model outputs, pre-period tests, bins, sample characteristics and numerical checks.
- `source_audit.json`, `cleaning_flow.csv`: input fingerprint, carrier parsing, sample counts and attrition.
- `route_features.csv`: all baseline eligibility, treatment, carrier-share coverage, concentration and support variables.
- `carrier_shares_2007.csv`: every recorded single-carrier code, including codes outside the legacy list.
- `panel_legacy.csv`, `panel_legacy_valid.csv`: reproduced and valid-distance samples.
- `panel_legacy_support.csv`, `panel_all_unexposed.csv`, `panel_all_support.csv`: exact alternative route-quarter panels.
- `support_*.csv`: joint-cell route counts and retention decisions.
- `event_coefficients.csv`, `event_comparison.png/.pdf`: saved event paths and generated figure.
- `independent_check_results.json`: separately reconstructed full-dummy validation.

The all-route intermediate `all_route_quarters.csv.gz` is regenerated and omitted from Git. Values called `revenue` are sums of sampled passenger counts times cell mean fares, not measured firm revenue. No field is evidence of physical nonstop service or complete ticket-level variation.

All pooled percentage changes use 100×[exp(β)−1]. Event coefficients and intervals remain log points. The early joint test uses 11 pre-reference coefficients from full-window event models with 2007Q4 as reference. These diagnostics and conventional sampling intervals do not establish causality.
