# Independent implementation review — September 27, 2026

Reviewed `repository/analysis/extend_analysis.py` against `analysis_plan.md`. This review does not edit the running implementation. The protocol's 750/1500/2000-mile bins, .1/.5/.9 share bins and minimum **three** routes per group per support cell govern this run; the earlier design-review suggestions were not a preregistration and do not supersede this protocol.

## Initial blocker reported to the implementer

The first run stopped in `read_source()` at the unconditional check that every eligible route's conditional single-carrier HHI falls between 0 and 10,000. Routes with no single-carrier journeys have an undefined conditional index, even if their aggregate market passenger count is sufficient for fare analysis. The correct QA distinguishes zero-denominator missingness from an out-of-range index with a positive denominator. Such routes should not be dropped from the fare analysis solely to make an HHI assertion pass. Positive-denominator failures require inspection of aggregation precision and carrier-share normalization. This was reported immediately; the cause and resolution must be recorded rather than merely weakening the assertion.

**Resolved:** the implementer retained these markets and restricted the valid-range assertion to positive denominators, separately asserting missing HHI for zero denominators. Independent saved-feature inspection confirms 19 of 5,535 eligible routes have zero single-carrier coverage. The canonical source restores 22 `cr1` and 20 `cr2` literal NA codes and contains 134 distinct named codes in their union. An unrelated old-panel key equality failure was a dtype mismatch (old int64 versus source int16), resolved by value comparison; it did not indicate different route-quarter keys.

## Design and estimator assessment

- The all-code single-carrier aggregation correctly combines one-coupon and same-code two-coupon traffic, then pools 2007 passengers before forming shares. Named mixed-carrier journeys are left out of the conditional index's denominator. The index is therefore conditional operating-code concentration, not complete firm-level concentration. The separate `delta_proxy` uses all-market denominators, while `delta_conditional` uses conditional denominators; do not describe the former as the increase in the reported conditional HHI. Dated regional/parent relationships and mixed itinerary allocation remain unresolved.
- Main bin covariates are fixed at 2007 values. The double panel merge appears deliberate: `one_share`/`distance`/`uaco` are route-quarter variables, whereas suffixed `_2007` variables are baseline characteristics. Regression uses saved baseline bin fields, not contemporaneous `one_share`. Assertions should explicitly verify those aliases, because the duplicate-looking names are easy to misuse in later tables.
- The additive distance/share × quarter model is not the same as joint-cell × quarter adjustment. The support specifications both change the route set and introduce joint-cell effects. Report those changes together, and do not attribute a movement exclusively to sample trimming. A same-support unadjusted regression would distinguish the components, if retained as an explicitly labeled additional diagnostic.
- Absorbing every column by route, then projecting outcome and target regressors on the same QR nuisance basis, is a correct Frisch–Waugh–Lovell implementation. The score sums from residualized target regressors yield the appropriate target block of the route-cluster covariance. The CR1 denominator `N-G-rank(nuisance)-K` correctly counts route intercepts and independent added regressors; the t reference is G−1.
- Pivoted QR deliberately removes redundant nuisance columns, including the common linear trend already spanned by quarter effects. This is acceptable because target regressors are kept separate and identified after projection. The pooled route-trend model avoids the saturated-event/path-trend collinearity described in the design review.
- The current rank check on residualized targets is relative to their own largest singular value. For a one-column target, numerical projection noise can formally have rank one even when the original regressor is effectively absorbed. Add a residualized-to-original target norm check and report its value, or use an absolute rank tolerance scaled to the unprojected target/design. This is a numerical safeguard; independent checks will establish whether the present estimates are affected.
- The event model uses 2007Q4 (t=12) as the omitted reference, and its joint test selects t<12. Therefore it does test all eleven earlier 2005–2007 relative gaps against that reference, without using an announcement-period reference. It is estimated within the full 2005–2010 event regression, not a separate regression restricted to 2005–2007. Do not inherit the earlier supplement's claim of a separate restricted-period model unless one is actually estimated.
- Joint event tests should explicitly check covariance rank and q≤G−1 before inversion. If a narrow support sample creates a singular test, return a clearly unavailable diagnostic rather than rely on a numerical inverse or promote a failure to reject. Small treated counts within support cells still constrain precision even when aggregate route count is large.
- Excluding every route with any 2007 UA/CO appearance is stricter than excluding sustained material exposure, but it is the announced rule and uses no post-focal-merger information. Apply it to both groups, report attrition and characterize it as a changed target population. Ending in 2009 changes follow-up length. Starting in 2006 avoids HP/US legal completion but does not establish that integration was finished or remove legacy-network shocks.
- No sign/significance-based branching was found. The median concentration split is determined using baseline exposure only. The main adjusted specifications do not control post-merger traffic, realized concentration, or itinerary composition. All three exposure models must be disclosed; an insignificant high-minus-low comparison is not evidence of no competition mechanism.

## Reproducibility gaps to address in accompanying materials

1. The protocol should say **2007 baseline** rather than “pre-2007” concentration.
2. `omit_transition` and `concentration_within_overlap` are additional model labels not separately enumerated in the protocol; acknowledge them as supplementary implementation checks rather than silently calling every model individually preregistered.
3. The CSV fallback preserves a literal `NA` if present, but cannot restore codes already blanked in the supplied CSV. Its audit label presently says canonical NBER archive regardless of input extension. Either require the canonical archive for this version, or distinguish fallback provenance and unknown-code handling explicitly.
4. Blank labels must never be silently treated as an independent carrier if a fallback CSV is allowed. Enforce named-carrier masks and export single-carrier coverage, residual mixed/unknown share and any zero-denominator counts. On the canonical archive, restored literal `NA` is a named carrier code.
5. The existing statsmodels check uses the implementation's returned within-transformed design. It is useful for FWL/covariance arithmetic but shares the feature-generation path. The independent checker should reconstruct raw dummy/interactions from saved panels and separately audit feature aliases and support membership.
6. Output metadata should record the script hash and source identity, and the archived protocol should travel with the replication package. Route-clustered intervals do not cover arbitrary airport/network dependence or identification bias.

## Numerical verification status

**Passed.** `independent_check.py` reconstructs raw route dummies, quarter dummies and bin/time interactions directly from complete saved panels, without importing the production estimator or its transformed design. It estimates full-dummy statsmodels regressions and compares target coefficients, route-clustered CR1 standard errors, residual degrees of freedom and the early-period Wald statistic. It also audits panel-key uniqueness, fare arithmetic, baseline aliases, bin membership, conditional carrier-share sums, HHI and exposure arithmetic, and exact three-per-group support membership. The results and reviewed script hash are saved in `independent_check_results.json`.

Seven complete-panel numerical replications passed: published baseline, distance-plus-composition adjustment, common-support cell effects, adjusted event study, route linear trends, ending in 2009 and excluding UA/CO. Across them, the largest absolute target-coefficient difference is 7.53e-13 and the largest clustered-SE difference is 1.11e-14. The adjusted early test independently gives F(11,259)=4.324397650004, p=6.14530245e-6. Every checked target retains substantive variation after nuisance projection (minimum regressor norm fraction at least .1868), so the current estimates are not affected by the numerical rank safeguard mentioned above. A statsmodels square-root warning arises on nuisance coefficient variances in the full trend design; the independently computed target estimate, target variance and degrees of freedom agree and remain finite.

## Interpretation and reporting checks from completed outputs

- Original legacy-based comparison routes **do include Southwest traffic**: 30 of105 controls have positive WN-coded traffic in2007. The rule requires a material combined legacy share; it does not ban low-cost-carrier presence. Expanding to all DL/NW-unexposed eligible routes adds routes failing the legacy screen; it is not simply “adding Southwest to an otherwise all-legacy market.” The expanded control set has460 routes,222 with observed WN; the support-restricted expanded controls have178 routes,119 with WN.
- Distance/composition adjustment moves the valid-distance sample contrast from −5.58% to −2.45%, with a95% interval of−5.92% to+1.14%. Its pre-period gaps still jointly reject stability. The result supports sensitivity to observed baseline composition, not proof that omitted composition caused all the original association or that the merger had no effect.
- The legacy common-support design retains133 treated and35 comparison routes (168total). It gives−1.80%, with interval−5.69% to+2.26%, and pretest p=.000388. Baseline fare, traffic and distance remain imperfectly balanced within the coarse cells. Joint-cell × quarter OLS creates its own conditional regression weighting; do not label this an ATT over all156 original overlap routes.
- Expanding the comparison population and restricting support retains146 treated and178 controls. Its−4.95% contrast remains negative, but the pretest p≈1.80e−9 prevents presenting this as a successfully repaired causal design. Report it alongside the narrower legacy-supported result, not as the selected answer.
- Excluding any baseline UA/CO appearance leaves only6 treated and33 controls. Its broad interval and changed population should accompany the estimate; it is not a decisive check that other mergers are irrelevant.
- Sustained one-coupon overlap under the5%-in3-of4 rule has **four** routes; the loose annual100-passenger rule has five. Neither is independently verified nonstop competition.
- Two routes in the expanded control samples have undefined conditional HHI because coverage is zero. Any mean HHI in those samples is among defined observations; show the number missing or define the denominator. No such missing HHI occurs in the legacy comparison sample.
- The high-minus-low exposure comparison is 0.02525 log points (SE .01777; p=.1566). The continuous mechanical-exposure coefficients are also imprecise. These proxies do not identify or reject a concentration mechanism, coordination, merger efficiencies, or welfare changes.

No remaining numerical blocker was found in the completed estimates. Documentation of proxy units, sample changes, sparse subgroups and the still-rejected pre-period comparisons is essential before publication.
